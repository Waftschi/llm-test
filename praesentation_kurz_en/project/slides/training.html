Learning

Training: guess, measure, nudge

1

Guess

The network computes an answer for all 230 examples.

2

Measure the error

Guess minus right answer: P − Y.

3

Distribute the blame

The error travels backwards to every weight (backpropagation).

4

Nudge

Every weight takes a small step against its blame.

new weight = old weight − learning rate × error × input

All of this 1,500 times. Start: random numbers. End: weights that fit the examples. Input = 1 if the word was in the sentence, otherwise 0.

The weights are not calculated but learned step by step. In each of the 1,500 rounds we guess, measure the error, distribute the blame backwards and move each weight a little in the direction that reduces the error.
