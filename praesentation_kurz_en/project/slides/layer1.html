A yes/no question about the sentence, for example: does kein occur together with bier? The network finds the questions itself.

Weight and bias

Learned numbers. A weight pushes a concept up (+) or down (−), the bias is the basic leaning.

tanh

Squeezes the sum to −1 (no) up to +1 (yes). Here +0.96: almost surely yes.

A real concept from the model, number 15. On the left the weights of the words in the sentence: bier pushes with plus 1.23, kein with plus 1.82, the context wein pulls down with minus 0.85. Sum plus bias gives 1.96, tanh turns that into 0.96. So this concept roughly asks: does kein occur together with bier? Nobody gave the network that question, it found it itself in training.
