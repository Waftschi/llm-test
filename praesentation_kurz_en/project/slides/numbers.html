Step 1

Text becomes numbers

Input x for “kein bier” in the context [wein]

einbierkeinwein[start][wein]…
011001…

141 columns, almost all 0. The context ([wein]) counts like a word.

Why numbers?

A computer only calculates with numbers. Every known word gets a 1 if it is in the sentence, otherwise a 0.

Unknown words

They are ignored. If more than half are unknown, the barkeeper asks back.

The sentence becomes a list of zeros and ones, one per known word. The context, i.e. what the guest ordered last, is treated like one more word. Nothing more happens in this step.
