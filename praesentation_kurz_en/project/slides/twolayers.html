Context

Why two layers?

One matrix

It only adds single-word votes. “kein” and “bier” cannot team up, so “kein bier” still votes for bier.

Two layers

A concept can fire only when kein and bier occur together, and then vote for wein.

tanh

Without tanh two layers would be mathematically just one matrix. Only tanh turns sums into switches, and switches allow AND and OR.

Command vergleich

One matrixTwo layers
Training examples223 / 230230 / 230
New sentences8 / 1212 / 12

New sentences like “bier bitte” or “heute ein wein” were not seen in training by either network.

The command vergleich trains a one-matrix network on the same examples for comparison. On the training examples it is almost as good, but on new sentences only 8 of 12 are right, while the two-layer network gets all 12. The reason: combinations like kein plus bier need a layer in between and the bend of tanh.
