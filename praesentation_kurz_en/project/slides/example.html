A chatbot that plays a bartender: sentence in, answer out.

Not a language model. It only has prepared answers and cannot write free text.

The same building blocks as an LLM: numbers, weights, softmax, learning from errors.

We type a sentence, the barkeeper answers. The second sentence is the important one: kein bier gives wein, the word kein flips the drink. The last sentence is nonsense, there the barkeeper asks back. Below is the size of the network: 230 training examples, 141 known words and contexts, 24 concepts, 34 answers, together 4,258 learned numbers.
