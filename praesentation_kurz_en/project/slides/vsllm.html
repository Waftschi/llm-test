What stays the same

Numbers, weights, softmax and learning from errors with backpropagation. Whoever understands the barkeeper understands the basic idea of an LLM.

The barkeeper is not a language model. He only picks from 34 prepared answers, knows 141 words and has 4,258 weights. An LLM predicts the next word, has billions of weights, uses attention to relate words to each other and was trained on huge amounts of text. The learning principle is the same.
