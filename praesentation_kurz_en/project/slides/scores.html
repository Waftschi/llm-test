Softmax

e to the power of (score − best score), then divide by the sum: percentages adding up to 100 %.

Four of the 24 concepts are shown, the other 20 are summed in one column. Each concept votes for or against each answer: weight times activation. All votes plus bias give the score, wein has 11.4 and bier 6.5. Softmax computes e to the power of the distance to the best score and divides by the sum, which gives 98.9 percent for wein. These are the numbers of the real model.
