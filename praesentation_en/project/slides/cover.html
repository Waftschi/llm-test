How a neural
network learns

The building blocks behind every LLM, shown on a small bartender that understands sentences and keeps learning

Team talk

Opening: the title deliberately does not say LLM, because our barkeeper is not a language model. It is a small neural network that maps a sentence to one of 34 answers. We are not looking at a real LLM but at a tiny neural network with about 4,000 weights. It uses the same basic ideas, but it is so small that we can see every single number. At the end everyone will know how a sentence becomes an answer and how the network learns. The example sentences are German, so a quick glossary: kein means no, bier means beer, wein means wine.
