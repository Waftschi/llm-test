What do the numbers mean?

c11, c5, c2, c15 are four of the 24 concepts from layer 1 (you know c15 from slide 5). Activation h is their tanh result for “kein bier”: +1 means yes, −1 means no. The numbers below are the concept’s votes for the answer. “20 more” sums up the remaining concepts so that Σ adds up.

Vote = weight W2[answer, concept] × activation h of the concept

Example c2 → wein: weight −3.31 × activation −1.00 = vote +3.31 (minus times minus: c2 says “no”, and “no” speaks for wein)

This continues slide 5: there concept 15 got the value h = +0.96. Here four concepts vote for the answers wein and bier. The activation h row shows how strongly each concept fires for kein bier (+1 yes, −1 no). Every number below is a vote: weight W2 times activation h. Concept c2, for example, is −1.00 (no) and has the weight −3.31 for wein, so −3.31 times −1.00 equals +3.31 for wein. The column 20 more sums up the remaining concepts, so Σ is the sum of all 24 votes. The bias of the answer is added, and the result is the score s: wein +11.4 against bier +6.5. These are exactly the numbers that softmax turns into percentages on slide 9. All numbers come from the current model for the sentence kein bier in the context wein (small deviations from rounding).
