Loss

How unsure the network is about the right answer: −ln(P of the right answer), averaged over all 230 examples. 0 would mean completely sure.

Hits

Share of the training examples where the right answer is in front. These are known examples, not new sentences.

Why 1500 rounds?

After 100 rounds all examples are right. After that the network only gets surer: “kein bier” goes from 88 % to 99 % wein.

These are real numbers from the barkeeper's training, seed 0, 230 examples. Round 0 is the start with random weights: the network guesses, the loss is high, and for kein bier it says wie geht es dir with 21 percent. After only 10 rounds about half of the examples are right, and kein bier moves towards wein. After 100 rounds all 230 training examples are right; after that the loss only falls slowly: the network gets surer, not more correct. Important: the hit rate applies to the training examples. How good the network is on new sentences is shown by the command vergleich. The loss is the average of minus the logarithm of the probability the network gave to the right answer. The loop from the previous slide, with the learning rule in every round, produced this result.
