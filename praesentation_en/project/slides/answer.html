The last step is simple: the percentages become an answer. The answer with the highest value wins; for kein bier that is wein with 98.9 percent. The barkeeper then picks one of the reply sentences stored for wein in the seed at random and prints it. The network itself does not write anything, it only chooses one of 34 prepared answers. There is a confidence threshold: if the best value is below 40 percent, hardly any words are known or more than half are unknown, the barkeeper says Hä? Wie bitte? instead. The threshold and the text are in the seed under fallback and can be changed. At the bottom the contrast once more: before learning the network did not know kein and answered bier, after learning it answers wein. The next slide sums up the whole way from text to answer.
