2. Divide by the sum: together 100 %.

Softmax turns raw scores into probabilities. The table shows our sentence “kein bier” in the context [wein] with the numbers of the current model. Softmax runs over all 34 answers, which is why the last row stands for the 31 others. We calculate with the gap to the best score; that gives the same percentages and avoids huge numbers: wein has e to the 11.4, which is about 85,000. The scores on the layer 2 slide (bier +8.4) come from an earlier run and do not match these numbers. The gap is what matters: with scores of 5 against 1 against 0 it would already be 98 %, 2 % and 1 %.
