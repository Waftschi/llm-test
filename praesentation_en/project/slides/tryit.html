Try it yourself

Start

python3 barkeeper2.py

python3 barkeeper2.py simple

python3 show_matrix.py

Everything runs in the console

Input: “ein bier”, “kein bier”, “noch eins” (a beer, no beer, one more)

Teach: “noch eins = bier” or “falsch” (wrong)

Log: concepts, sums, tanh and softmax via the command log

Mini model: just 4 concepts and 43 weights

Questions?

A final hint for trying it out. If you want to follow the numbers yourself, start the mini model with only four concepts and have show_matrix.py display the matrix after every sentence you teach. In the console, the decision log under every answer shows the whole calculation of this presentation with the real numbers.
