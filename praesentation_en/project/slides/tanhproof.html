Putting it in context – mini example

It does not work without tanh

With tanh – 4 of 4 correct

Sentencec0c1wein→
bier−0.91−1.00+0.19bier ✓
wein+0.91−0.91+3.62wein ✓
kein bier+0.91−0.91+3.62wein ✓

Without tanh – 2 of 4 correct

Sentencec0c1wein→
bier−1.5−4.5+6.00wein ✗
wein+1.5−1.5+6.00wein ✓
kein bier+1.5−1.5+6.00wein ✓
kein wein+4.5+1.5+6.00wein ✗

wein = 1 if the word is in the sentence, otherwise 0. The same for kein. “3·wein” means: 3 points if wein occurs. c0 and c1 are the two switches from slide 23: c0 = OR (on from one word), c1 = AND (on only with both).

2·(3·wein + 3·kein − 1.5) − 2·(3·wein + 3·kein − 4.5) = 6  for every sentence

The word points (3·wein + 3·kein) appear in both brackets and drop out. What is left: 2·(−1.5) − 2·(−4.5) = −3 + 9 = 6. Also: wein points = 2·c0 − 2·c1, bier points always 1. That is why wein always wins.

Without tanh the words cancel out: two layers are then just one matrix. Only tanh turns the sums into switches (on/off), and only switches allow AND and OR.

The same mini network as on the previous slide, once with and once without tanh. On the left, with tanh: all four sentences are correct. On the right, without tanh, the raw sums go straight into layer 2. Then every sentence gets exactly 6 wein points, and the barkeeper always says wein. The calculation below shows why: the word parts cancel out exactly, and only the difference between the two hurdles is left. This is not bad luck with the numbers, it holds for any weights: without the bend, two matrices can be multiplied into one single matrix, and one matrix can only add up votes. tanh bends the sum: from about plus or minus 2 the concept is practically fully on or fully off. The sum becomes a switch, and switches can be combined into AND and OR. A second benefit: the values stay between minus 1 and plus 1, even for very long sentences. "kein kein kein wein wein wein" would have the sum 16.5 without tanh and drown out everything else; with tanh it stays at 1.00. Large LLMs mostly use GELU or ReLU instead of tanh; the job is the same.
