Putting it in context

From the barkeeper to an LLM

AspectBarkeeperLLM
Weightsabout 4,000billions
Input1 column per wordembeddings (meaning vectors)
Taskpick 1 of 34 answerspredict the next token
Data230 examplestrillions of tokens
Layers2dozens to hundreds

Unchanged: random start, softmax, measuring the error and improving the weights by gradient.

Besides size, the most important difference is the input. In the barkeeper every word is its own column with no relationship to the others. An LLM learns embeddings: words with similar usage, such as “kein” and “no”, get similar vectors, and everything learned about one word also applies to the other. Also, an LLM predicts the next word instead of picking a fixed answer out of 34. The learning mechanics, however, are the same as in this talk.
