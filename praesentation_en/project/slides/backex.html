A tiny example with three answers and two concepts. The network guesses wein with 61 percent, but the right answer is bier. Step 1: D2 is guess minus correct answer. Bier is far too weak with minus 0.74, wein far too strong with plus 0.61. Step 2: we pass these errors back through the weights W2 to the concepts. For every concept, all answer errors are multiplied by that concept’s weight into the respective answer and added up. Concept 0 votes strongly for wein with weight 1.5 and therefore receives a lot of blame, plus 0.56. Step 3: the factor 1 minus h squared is the slope of tanh. Concept 0 has activation 0.9, so it sits close to the limit and its factor is only 0.19, which means it is hard to move. Concept 1 is in the middle at minus 0.5, so its factor is 0.75. In the end both concepts have the same blame, 0.11. From this the weight changes are calculated, which the next slide shows.
