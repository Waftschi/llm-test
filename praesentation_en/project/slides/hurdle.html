Putting it in context – mini example

Weight = points, bias = hurdle

Barkeeper: “Bier oder Wein?”

Guest saysgets
bierbier
weinwein
kein bierwein
kein weinbier

Only two choices (beer or wine): “kein” (no) flips the drink. The network does not know the question, it only sees these four examples.

Each of the words wein and kein brings 3 points. The bias is the hurdle: if the sum is above it, the concept is on.

0 words: 0│1.5│1 word: 3│4.5│2 words: 6

Points before the hurdle – the hurdles sit exactly in the middle

c0 = tanh(3·wein + 3·kein − 1.5)

−1.5 / +1.5 / +4.5

On from one word: OR

c1 = tanh(3·wein + 3·kein − 4.5)

−4.5 / −1.5 / +1.5

On only with both: AND

wein points = 2·c0 − 2·c1; bier points = 1

OR but not AND (= XOR): that is how “kein bier” becomes wein

A mini network with only two concepts; the numbers are chosen by hand so that every step can be checked. The situation: the barkeeper asks "Bier oder Wein?", beer or wine. Because there are only two choices, "kein bier", no beer, means wine, and "kein wein" means beer. The network does not know this question; the knowledge is only in the four examples. The word bier needs no weight of its own: if wein is not in the sentence, it was beer. Now the meaning of the numbers. Each word brings 3 points, so the possible sums are 0, 3 or 6. The bias is a hurdle that is subtracted. tanh then only looks at whether the rest is positive, then the concept is on, or negative, then it is off. Concept c0 has the hurdle 1.5, one word is already enough to clear it: c0 means "wein or kein". Concept c1 has the hurdle 4.5, only both words together clear it: c1 means "wein and kein". Same weights, just a different hurdle, and an OR becomes an AND. The hurdles sit exactly in the middle between the point values, so every sentence is clearly on or off. The weights of 3 are big enough for tanh to come close to plus or minus 1. Layer 2 then computes wein points as 2 times c0 minus 2 times c1, that is OR but not AND. The technical term is XOR, the classic example a single matrix cannot learn. In the real barkeeper nobody chooses these numbers, training finds weights and hurdles by itself. Weights of about 3 to 4 in the log, for example bier plus 3.9, are the counterparts of these 3s.
